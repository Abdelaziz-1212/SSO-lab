IEEE 33-bus feeder: where must the PMUs go?

Tap a bus to add or remove a PMU. A PMU measures its bus voltage and the current of every line at that bus. Meet all three requirements with as few PMUs as you can. The example starts with one PMU at each candidate plant bus.

Detection counts a PMU when the oscillation it measures exceeds its noise floor, 3.2 × 10⁻⁴ of the fundamental (SNR 50 dB). "At any bus" uses an exact network model with a source at every bus, 5 to 45 Hz, eight network configurations and a series capacitor case. Each requirement asks for two PMUs, so one can fail. Use Fail a PMU, then tap a PMU, to see what one PMU still guarantees.
